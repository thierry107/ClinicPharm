About Curis Health

Curis Health is a dedicated healthcare management platform built to bridge the gap between clinical consultations and pharmacy dispensing. Designed specifically for health providers across Kenya and East Africa.

Core Management Modules

Designed with dedicated interfaces for Patients, Doctors/Staff, and System Administrators.

Clinical Consultation

Doctor records consultation findings, vital signs, and issues prescriptions directly to the chemist queue.

Appointment Management

Schedule appointments, assign medical practitioners, and manage patient queues seamlessly.

Prescription Management

Track prescription status from issuance to chemist dispensing and patient collection.

Chemist Inventory

Real-time stock monitoring, batch numbers, expiry dates, low-stock alerts, and supplier re-orders.

POS & Billing

Fast barcode & search checkout, tax/discount calculation, MPesa payment simulation, and printable receipts.

Business Analytics

Track gross sales, prescription fulfillment rates, top prescribed medications, and patient growth.

Pharmaceutical Catalogue

Medicine catalogue connected to chemist inventory with real-time stock indicators in Kenyan Shillings (KSh).
